What the market is telling us

One week of data, read together.

Our home launched into an unusual week. The Federal Reserve raised its benchmark rate by a quarter point at its September 15 to 16 meeting, its first increase since July 2023, and Freddie Mac's average 30-year mortgage rate rose to 6.95% on September 17, up from 6.76% the week before. Jumps like this often cause buyers to pause and re-run their numbers before acting.

Property strengths

  • Strong early attention: 409 Zillow views, 45 saves, and 856 views across three sites in seven days.
  • An 11.0% Zillow save rate, second only to a comparable home that went under contract in its second week.
  • Professional photos, a 3D tour, and pre-launch prep that are clearly resonating with the buyers who find the listing.
  • Features that set it apart on this floor plan: hardwood floors, a primary ensuite, and a finished, heated and cooled garage.
  • No HOA, true one-level living, and an easy walk to Thornton Community Park and the city pool.

Market considerations

  • Rates rose during our launch week, which may slow some buyers' decisions in the short term.
  • Fewer shoppers are clicking in than on the two newest comparable homes, possibly because $420,000 sits above common search limits like $400,000.
  • Buyers are comparing condition closely against renovated homes selling between $415,000 and $430,000.
  • New same-size competition at $400,000 arrived today (9051 Lilly Ct), joining 9390 Nagel Dr at the same price.

Our Recommendation

Hold steady and give the market time to respond.

Our recommendation is to stay at $420,000 and be patient for now.

The first week gave us encouraging signals: strong online interest, three showings, and a save rate that compares well with nearby homes, including one that sold quickly. It also coincided with a rate increase that has likely caused some buyers to pause. We think it makes sense to let that settle before drawing conclusions from one week and one feedback response.

Next week we will be watching whether our daily Zillow views and saves hold or pick up, whether showing requests keep coming, and whether other buyers raise the same condition and price comparison. As always, this is a decision we make together, and we are happy to talk through any questions.

Next steps

  1. Keep the list price at $420,000.
  2. Continue monitoring online activity, showing requests, and buyer feedback as it comes in.
  3. Track these same competing listings on Zillow each week so we can compare new views and saves side by side.
  4. Deliver next week's update with the first week-over-week comparison of views, saves, and showings.
